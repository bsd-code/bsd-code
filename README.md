## 👋 About me

I'm **MrSavvy**, a student of **DAM (Multiplatform Application Development)**, building my skills through coursework, practical exercises and personal projects.

My main interest is **Android development with Kotlin**. I like understanding why things work—not just getting code to run—and improving through small projects, debugging, testing and documentation.

- 📱 **Current focus:** Kotlin, Android development, Android UI, and Firebase integration.
- 🧩 **Learning approach:** build, test, debug, document, and iterate.
- 🎓 **Looking for:** FCT internship opportunities and opportunities to collaborate on learning-focused projects.
- 🌱 **Open-source goal:** prepare to make my first meaningful contributions.
